Feature pamphlet

Leased Initiative

Run the full lease lifecycle — from sign-up and approvals to signed leases, rent, and maintenance.

One dual-sided app. Landlords work in the studio. Tenants work in the portal. Same source of truth.

Tenant Pipeline

Keep every applicant moving — no more “where did they go?”

  • Invite by link or one-time code (texted to their phone)
  • Public Discovery or Invite-Only — you choose how tenants find you
  • Waiting to Connect → Pending → Official, with clear next steps
  • Accept & Draft Lease in one move
  • Occupancy tags: entire home, roommates, private or shared room
  • Add tenants manually or send invite shortcuts from the dashboard
  • Archive finished leases to Past Tenants — or delete when you’re done

Rentals & Portfolio

Your addresses, beds, and openings — organized like inventory.

  • Add rentals with furnished status, room/bed pricing, deposits, and utilities
  • Max occupancy from beds; total rent and full-occupancy cost for applications
  • Color-coded occupancy: open beds vs. full homes at a glance
  • Expand a rental to see who’s there; open Tenant Details from a name
  • Assign tenants to bedroom and bed
  • Filter by state, town, and custom groups (including map radius)
  • Tile or Spreadsheet View, with editable columns and tile size
  • Upcoming Openings: vacant units and leases ending soon
  • Send Re-sign Message or Generate Invite Code from each row

Lease Agreements

Draft, send, sign, restyle — without losing the paper trail.

  • Draft from Accept & Draft Lease or your default template
  • Preview, download, upload a replacement, then send when ready
  • Optional Auto-send from Pending Tenants
  • Tenants review and sign with a drawn signature
  • Sent / Signed status and term progress on every agreement
  • Upload PDF or Word templates; Confirm as default
  • Apply a new style to pending or official tenants — details and signatures stay intact
  • Seasonal options, custom lease eras, payment terms, and footers
  • Import existing leases → Scan → Add to Official Tenants or Confirm to Pending

Payments & Money Flow

Deposits, rent, balances, and overdue — all in one place.

  • Deposit invoice and payment link auto-appear after signing
  • Pay with PayPal, Stripe, or Square
  • Payment Status shows Deposit Paid with the processor logo
  • Confirm Payment Complete: Awaiting Deposit → Upcoming → Active
  • Track unit rent, roommate share, balance, and next due date
  • Filter by Overdue Rent, Paid Early, or payment method
  • Tenants pay anytime — including months upfront when allowed
  • Overdue tag jumps straight to that tenant’s overdue payments

Outreach & Reminders

Nudge overdue rent without leaving the app — replies stay on your phone.

  • Send Message to Tenant from Payments with templates or your own copy
  • Overdue rent messaging that keeps the conversation on SMS
  • Client Automation for overdue rent and lease milestone nudges
  • Tenant alerts by email and in-portal when something needs attention

Maintenance & Alerts

Photo in. Problem clear. Dispatch ready.

  • Tenants Request Maintenance with a required photo and optional note
  • Requests land in Tenant Alerts (desktop nav or mobile Menu)
  • Assess and dispatch from one focused queue

Tenant Portal

Apply, sign, pay, share, and stay on schedule — from one home base.

  • Start Application: landlord, address, furnished bed/room, roommate preference
  • Or join with an invite link/code and a pre-filled form
  • Dashboard: rent due dates, Pay Rent, leases, and deposit invoices
  • Review and sign lease agreements in-portal
  • Share files and short notes with your landlord
  • Follow lease milestones on the project timeline
  • Choose Style and manage My profile from Menu
  • Restart the product tour anytime

Views, Filters & Clarity

See what matters — hide what doesn’t.

  • Spreadsheet or Tile View for tenants, leases, rentals, and payments
  • Edit Columns: rearrange, hide, or restore fields
  • Filter Official Tenants by lease progress and building type
  • Filter leases by status, progress, state, area code, or group
  • Show Occupancy Status tags under tenant names when you want them
  • Highlight last import after bringing in existing leases
  • Mobile-friendly tiles; Display Settings on larger screens

Company, Settings & Style

Set the defaults once. Run the portfolio every day.

  • Company Profile: name, rental and renter counts, Past Tenants (Archived)
  • Business Information for lease headers and contact details
  • Lease Defaults and Lease Agreement Templates in one Settings hub
  • App Style previews that apply instantly to the landlord studio
  • In-app tour with section jump bar — restart from Menu anytime
  • Demo mode with host access codes and landlord/tenant POV switching (changes aren’t saved)

Brand values

  • Fair — Both sides share the same information.
  • Practical — Import leases, track overdue rent, message in place.
  • Accountable — Signatures and payments leave a trail.
  • Calm — Clean UI, focus on the work.
